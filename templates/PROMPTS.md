# OpenArt prompts: shaadi card backgrounds

## Har tasveer/video ke liye zaroori baatein
- **Size:** vertical **9:16** (720×1280 ya 1080×1920)
- **Tasveer mein koi text, naam ya likhai NAHI honi chahiye.** Text website khud likhegi.
- **Text ki jagah khaali rakhein:**
  - Shahi aur Gulabi: upar wala aadha hissa saaf rahe (aasman ya halki deewar)
  - Mehndi: beech ka hissa saaf rahe
- **Video:** 5–10 second, dheemi harkat (phool hilein, mombattiyan timtimayein, kapra lehraye), aur loop ho sake
- **File ka naam:**
  - `templates/<naam>/bg.mp4` (video) ya `templates/<naam>/bg.jpg` (tasveer)
  - Design chunne wale card ke liye ek chhoti tasveer: `templates/<naam>/thumb.jpg`

Negative prompt (sab ke liye): `text, letters, words, watermark, logo, signature, frame border, blurry, distorted faces, extra fingers`
